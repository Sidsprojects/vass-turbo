02 THE STORY

From Alang
to the world.

Old steel.
New purpose.

VESS TANKO was established in 2016 to serve ship technical management companies worldwide.

We source machinery and equipment from Alang–Bhavnagar, inspect every component, and turn proven equipment into dependable working assets. From main and auxiliary engine spares to pumps, hydraulic systems, anchors and chains, our work is built around practical engineering and export-ready execution.
